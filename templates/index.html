Why it doesn't look embedded

Classic LSB steganography equalises histogram value-pairs — the exact signature the chi-square pairs-of-values attack (Westfeld–Pfitzmann) is built to catch. This encoder adds a small continuous residual instead, so the histogram barely moves.
